About Us

About Five Hills Business Solutions LLC

Who We Are

Rooted in Central Texas, Five Hills Business Solutions LLC—evolving from the strong foundation of Five Hills Investing—is a full-service business consultancy and advisory firm based in Killeen, Texas. Founded and led by Eugene Alexander, our firm is dedicated to equipping entrepreneurs, small-to-midsize business owners, and commercial operators with the strategic insight, practical resources, and execution needed to achieve sustainable growth.

What began with a sharp focus on real estate investment and capital strategy has naturally expanded. As our clients' needs evolved, so did we. Today, Five Hills Business Solutions LLC delivers comprehensive end-to-end guidance, helping businesses streamline operations, access capital, optimize resources, and navigate complex market landscapes.

Our Mission

Actionable Business Solutions. Sustainable Growth.

To deliver actionable, high-impact business solutions that empower local and regional enterprises to build equity, operate efficiently, and achieve long-term financial independence.

Leadership

Eugene Alexander

Founder & Managing Partner

As the Founder and Managing Partner, Eugene Alexander brings years of direct market experience, business discipline, and strategic vision to Five Hills Business Solutions LLC. With deep roots across the Killeen, Fort Cavazos (Fort Hood), and Greater Bell County communities, Eugene founded the original Five Hills Investing with a clear principle: disciplined strategy creates lasting value.

Eugene’s hands-on approach centers on partnership and accountability. Whether guiding a startup through its initial structuring phase or advising established companies on scaling their operations, Eugene works alongside leadership teams to demystify complex business challenges and drive measurable outcomes.
