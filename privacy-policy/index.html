Privacy Policy

Five Hills Business Solutions LLC ("Five Hills Business Solutions," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through our website.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide through our website, including your name, phone number, email address, business information, and information submitted through contact or inquiry forms.

How We Use Your Information

We may use information you provide to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests.
  • Schedule and manage calls or appointments.
  • Provide information about requested services.
  • Communicate about accounts or services.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Send SMS communications when you have provided appropriate consent.

SMS Communications

If you provide your phone number and opt in to SMS communications, Five Hills Business Solutions LLC may send text messages regarding your inquiry, call scheduling, account, and services.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP.

SMS consent is not a condition of purchase.

We do not sell or share your phone number or SMS opt-in information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Information Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our website, communications, scheduling, customer support, or business services. These providers may only use information as necessary to provide services to us.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, or business.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to help protect information submitted through our website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request information about personal information we maintain about you or to request that we update or correct your information, subject to applicable law.

For SMS communications, reply STOP to opt out or HELP for support.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Five Hills Business Solutions LLC through the contact information provided on our website.
